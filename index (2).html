Hypertension

hy·per·ten·sion

lightbulb The Simple Meaning

Hypertension is simply the medical word for high blood pressure. It means the force of your blood pushing against your blood vessel walls is consistently too high, making your heart work harder than it should.

What you should know:

  • check
    It often has no symptoms, which is why it's sometimes called a "silent condition."
  • check
    A normal blood pressure reading is usually around 120/80. If yours is often higher, your doctor might diagnose hypertension.
  • check
    It can be managed well with lifestyle changes (like less salt and more exercise) and medication.
